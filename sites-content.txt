Career Highlights
Background
With 2 decade+ years in the technology industry, first I began my career as a system software developer working on client/server and middleware applications, including protocol development. Over time I transitioned into leadership, bridging deep engineering and strategic oversight.

My journey spans building high-performance trading platforms (HFT/market-data) to today’s cutting-edge domains of Gen AI, full-stack development and autonomous systems. I’ve architected resilient solutions, modernised legacy systems and enabled teams to deliver at scale. As a hands-on architect fluent in full-stack development, DevOps, cloud and modular system design, I’ve led cross-discipline teams, mentored engineers into leadership roles and driven measurable impact—whether latency reductions, throughput gains or operational excellence. I remain passionate about emerging domains, technical innovation and continuous delivery of business value.

At Persistent Systems, in my role as Principal Architect, I’m contributing to multiple Gen AI initiatives for a UK-based financial organization—automating KTLO activities, enabling UI/UX auto-generation and scaling AI-enabled platforms. I’m also architecting a major healthcare transformation: migrating from a Salesforce/UI stack to a standalone React/PostgreSQL platform on AWS, with AI-assisted case review workflows. In addition I led the full-stack design of an internal Gen AI “ChatGPT-style” assistant—leveraging retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), vector databases, prompt engineering and cloud-scale deployment. I further oversaw integration of a Gen AI-Hub product into a client’s ecosystem—covering LLM apps, key-management, evaluation frameworks and custom cloud services.

My strength lies in marrying technical mastery (modern frameworks, cloud infrastructure, DevOps practices) with leadership—mentoring teams, defining architecture, aligning with business goals and delivering outcomes at scale.
